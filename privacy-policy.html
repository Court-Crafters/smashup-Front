Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Welcome to Padel4Passion, the all-in-one platform for players, coaches, and clubs passionate about padel. Protecting your privacy is one of our core values.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Padel4Passion collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information when you access our app, website, or related services. By using our platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We are committed to handling your data responsibly and transparently, ensuring that your experience remains safe, personalized, and enjoyable.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our platform, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Data: such as your full name, email address, phone number, and profile photo when you register.
  • Usage Data: including match history, chat messages, favorite clubs, and browsing behavior within the app.
  • Location Data: to suggest nearby courts, matches, and coaches (only if you allow location access).
  • Device Information: such as your device type, operating system, and app version for performance optimization.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your data to:

  • Enable user registration, profile setup, and matchmaking.
  • Provide personalized recommendations for matches and events.
  • Facilitate communication between players, coaches, and clubs.
  • Improve app functionality, design, and user experience.
  • Send notifications, offers, or news (only with your consent).
  • Ensure security, prevent fraud, and maintain service integrity.

We may also anonymize certain data for analytics and performance reporting to better understand how users interact with Padel4Passion.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal data. However, we may share limited information with:

  • Service providers that help us host, process, or analyze data (e.g., cloud hosting or analytics tools).
  • Business partners for tournaments, clubs, and sponsor-related collaborations, only with your consent.
  • Authorities if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Every partner we work with is required to comply with strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.

5. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You can request account deletion at any time, and your data will be permanently removed within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your personal information. You can:

  • Access and update your personal data.
  • Request the deletion of your account and related data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing or notifications.
  • Ask for a copy of your stored data in digital format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@padel4passion.com.

7. Data Security

Your trust is our priority. We use encryption, access controls, and secure communication protocols to protect your personal information. While no system is 100% immune to threats, we constantly update our security practices to minimize risks.

8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in different countries where our technical partners are located. We ensure that such transfers comply with data protection laws and maintain the same high level of security.

9. Children’s Privacy

Padel4Passion is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us to remove it.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. All changes will be published here, and the “Last updated” date will be revised. Please review this page periodically.
